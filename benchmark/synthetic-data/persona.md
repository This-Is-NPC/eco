# Sadao Maia

Fictional persona for eco's benchmark: a demon lord in exile who wants to be
promoted, inspired by Sadao Maou of *Hataraku Maou-sama!* (2013). Every fact
below is invented; answers about Sadao may use only these facts, each within
its own case.

Tech lead, .NET and applied AI, aiming to become a manager. São Paulo,
Brazil. Portuguese (fluent, works in it every day), English (fluent, worked in
English with clients for four years).

Stack: C#, .NET 8, SQL Server, PostgreSQL, Azure, Python. Has never used DB2
or a mainframe. Has never run Kubernetes in production; he only studied it on
a local cluster.

## MagRonald's Tecnologia (2024 – now)

Tech lead at MagRonald's Tecnologia, the IT arm of the MagRonald's burger
chain.

- Put three AI agents in production that prepare the monthly operations
  report for the chain's 11 regions.
- The report cycle went from 9 days to 2 days.
- The agents reach data only through tools the team wrote, read-only.

## Santa Luz Saúde (2021 – 2023)

Senior developer, then lead of a team of 6 developers, at a regulated
healthcare company.

- Every system went through formal validation before production, under the
  health regulator's rules.
- Built a deploy pipeline with two required approvals and an audit trail of
  every change.
- The company passed 2 external audits with zero findings on his systems.

## Caronte Logística (2019 – 2021)

Developer in a team of 4 at a river freight company.

- Moved the business rules of 23 SQL Server stored procedures into C#, one
  procedure at a time, comparing old and new results before switching.
- The nightly freight batch went from 4 hours to 50 minutes.

## Sasazuka project, Japan (2023, three months)

Exchange program with a Japanese manufacturer.

- Split a WPF desktop app that was one giant file into the MVVM pattern.
- The codebase shrank by 37%.
- The Japanese team resisted AI tools; he ran 20-minute pairing sessions
  that used AI on the chores they disliked most (test data, boilerplate).

## The cast

Everyone else in the scenarios. All of them are fictional.

- **Gertrudes**, the goblin recruiter of the Adventurers' Guild, which hires a
  tech lead for its dungeon system; she runs every round of Sadao's interview
  (scenarios 1–6). Goblins and adventurers' guilds of *Dungeons & Dragons*.
- **Brasa**, the fire demon who runs MagRonald's Tecnologia's infrastructure
  from a walking castle, so plans change when the castle moves (7, 8, 11, 13,
  and the client meeting of 14). Homage to Calcifer of *Howl's Moving Castle*
  (Diana Wynne Jones, 1986; Studio Ghibli, 2004).
- **Alcides**, **Lúcio** and **Emília**, Brasa's team: Sadao's frugal general,
  now on data; a fallen angel on the platform who never leaves his chair; and
  the hero who followed Sadao to keep an eye on him (Alcides in 7, Lúcio in 8,
  all three in 13).
- **Bartolomeu**, the vampire who owns HemoNoite, a chain of 24-hour blood
  banks: the foreign client, a polyglot after centuries (12, 14–19). Homage to
  *Dracula* (Bram Stoker, 1897).
- **Kevin (K-9)**, the robot English teacher with impostor syndrome, who
  doubts his own rules (9). Homage to K-9 of *Doctor Who* (BBC, 1977).
- Sadao alone in the idea session, where his plans of world domination turned
  corporate (10).
